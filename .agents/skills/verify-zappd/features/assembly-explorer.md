# Assembly explorer

Assembly explorer renders any of the seven Zappd CAD assemblies with baked PBR material skins, KiCad PCBs, COTS batteries, and OpenCascade-routed 3D cables in a full-viewport Three.js WebGL studio canvas behind a floating HUD with a top-right explode toggle, continuous explode slider, conditional 3-state wiring and shell visibility cycle buttons, zoom icon buttons, overview hover inspection with electrical circuit trace highlighting, click-to-focus smooth glide with a top-center `Back` button and 3D-anchored part callout, parametric rebuild controls on Helix Config, unverified warnings and partial CAD inspection panels on Poppy Mark I, and a floating bottom prompt composer.

## Sub-features

- `explorer-3d-render` loads the GLB into a full-bleed WebGL studio canvas with geometry-driven camera framing (`webglActive === true`, `glbNodeCount === nodeCount`) across all seven catalog assemblies: `quadcopter_drone_v1` (`Aero X4`, `42` nodes, `composed_validated`, `~0.80 m`), `robotic_arm_3dof_v1` (`Kinetix 5`, `31` nodes, `composed_validated`, `~0.79 m`), `open_source_rover_v1` (`Nova II`, `21` nodes, `composed_validated`, `1` shell, `~0.72 m`), `open_quadruped_v1` (`Strider Q4`, `40` nodes, `composed_validated`, `~1.52 m`), `parametric_multirotor_v1` (`Helix Config`, `38` default nodes, `composed_validated`, `4` parameters, `~0.86 m`), `reachy_mini_v1` (`Reachy Mini`, `98` nodes, `composed_validated`, `10` shells, `~1.30 m`), and `poppy_humanoid_v1` (`Poppy Mark I`, `289` nodes, `unverified`, `~3.14 m`).
- `explorer-hover-inspect` hovers a 3D piece in overview (`pointerType: "mouse"`) to highlight the piece in gold, dim unrelated parts, show `status "Hovered component"` (`hoveredComponentId !== null`, `hoveredPartCallout !== null`), and highlight connected electrical peers and cables in cyan (`circuitHighlightIds.length > 0`) when hovering an electronics, power, or wiring node.
- `explorer-explode-hud` toggles `Explode (0%)` to `Explode (65%)` (`explodeFactor === 0.65`) and back to `0%` (or disables as `Explode unavailable` if no part has `explodeDistanceMeters > 0`), supports continuous dragging via the `Explode factor` slider, and zooms via icon-only `Zoom out` and `Zoom in` buttons.
- `explorer-wiring-visibility` renders on assemblies with wiring nodes and cycles through `Wiring visibility: Auto` (`wiringVisibility === "auto"`, auto-fading untraced cables across `explodeFactor 0.25..0.55` on open-frame assemblies and `0.48..0.72` on shell assemblies), `Wiring visibility: Shown` (`wiringVisibility === "show"`, `aria-pressed="true"`, label `"Wiring on"`), and `Wiring visibility: Hidden` (`wiringVisibility === "hide"`, label `"Wiring off"`).
- `explorer-shell-xray-peel` renders a 3-state `Shell` cycle button only on assemblies with `enclosureRole: "shell"` parts (`hasShell === true`, `open_source_rover_v1` and `reachy_mini_v1`), cycling `Shell visibility: Solid` (`"solid"`), `Shell visibility: X-ray` (`"xray"`, translucent click-through shell letting hover and click focus internal parts in place at `explodeFactor === 0`), and `Shell visibility: Hidden` (`"hidden"`), while staging `0%..40%` explode to peel outer shells before internal parts separate across `40%..100%`.
- `explorer-part-focus` clicks a 3D piece on the canvas to explode the assembly to at least `65%` when `shellVisibility === "solid"` (or preserve `explodeFactor` in place when `shellVisibility` is `"xray"` or `"hidden"`), isolate the part (`isolatedComponentId === selectedComponentId`), glide the camera smoothly onto the piece (`1.1 s`), highlight connected electrical peers and cables in cyan for electronics/power nodes (`circuitHighlightIds`), show the top-center `Back` button, and render a live 3D-anchored `status "Focused component"` callout (`viewMode === "part_focus"`, `focusedPartCallout !== null`).
- `explorer-back-restore` clicks the top-center `Back` button (or presses `Escape`) to glide back (`0.65 s`) and restore the overview camera and previous explode factor (`viewMode === "overview"`, `selectedComponentId === null`, `isolatedComponentId === null`, `focusedPartCallout === null`).
- `explorer-parametric-controls` renders an open `<details aria-label="Assembly parameters">` panel on `parametric_multirotor_v1` (`Helix Config`) with integer stepper controls (`Decrease Rotor Count`, `Increase Rotor Count`), float range sliders (`Wheelbase`, `Deck Gap`, `Arm Thickness`), and `button "Apply parameters"`, rebuilding geometry in place via `POST /api/rebuild` and updating `manifestHash`, `parameterValues`, and `buildCacheMode`.
- `explorer-unverified-and-source-inspection` renders `alert "Unverified assembly warning"` with `list "Failed validation checks"` whenever `verificationStatus === "unverified"`, and renders the partial CAD inspection status panel (`"Partial CAD inspection · engineering unverified"`, `"Inspection validation limits"`, and `"Source component hierarchy"` with clickable `button "Select source component <id>"` rows) on `poppy_humanoid_v1` (`Poppy Mark I`).
- `explorer-mobile-layout` keeps the full-viewport 3D canvas, top-right HUD bar shifted below `Back to assemblies`, part-focus callout, and floating bottom composer usable at `430x932` without horizontal overflow (`overflowX === false`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose any of the seven catalog cards on `/` (`Aero X4`, `Kinetix 5`, `Nova II`, `Strider Q4`, `Helix Config`, `Reachy Mini`, `Poppy Mark I`) or open `/assemblies/<archetypeId>` directly.
- Move the pointer over 3D parts in overview to inspect part names and electrical circuit highlights.
- Click `Explode (0%)`, drag `Explode factor`, click `Wiring`, click `Shell` (on Nova II or Reachy Mini), click `Zoom in` or `Zoom out`, drag the canvas to orbit, click any 3D piece to focus it, and click `Back` (or press `Escape`) to return to overview.
- On `Helix Config`, adjust `Parameters` and click `Apply`. On `Poppy Mark I`, inspect the unverified warning and expandable inspection panels.

## Gotchas

- `cdp-browser.mjs` launches Chrome with `--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader` and background-throttling disabled at `deviceScaleFactor: 1` so headless WebGL renders shaded PBR GLB frames reliably. If `diagnostics.assembly.webglActive` is `false`, inspect `chrome.log`.
- `diagnostics.assembly.nodeScreenPositions` stores each component's projected screen point as a 2-element array `[x, y]` (not `{ x, y }`). Wait for `browser-wait --settled` before reading coordinates for canvas hovers and clicks, and avoid clicking while an explode spring is still animating.
- Focus transitions glide smoothly (`1.1 s` focus-in, `0.65 s` focus-out), while `diagnostics.assembly.camera` reports the target camera immediately. Waiting for `--learning-ready explanation` (or opening Chrome with `--reduced-motion true`) avoids capturing an in-flight camera frame in the PNG.
- Overview `camera.distanceMeters` is computed dynamically from the assembly's leaf bounding box (`~0.80 m` Aero X4, `~0.79 m` Kinetix 5, `~0.72 m` Nova II, `~1.52 m` Strider Q4, `~0.86 m` Helix Config, `~1.30 m` Reachy Mini, `~3.14 m` Poppy Mark I); `0.38 m` is only the pre-load fallback when `assembly === null`.
- `diagnostics.assembly.unverifiedFailures` exports `assembly.validation.failures`, while the rendered `Unverified assembly warning` DOM list also includes `electricalFailures` and `Floating component <id>` entries.
- Coordinate `hover` and `click` actions (`--x <x> --y <y>`) dispatch real CDP mouse events into the Three.js raycaster on the `<canvas>`. Always verify the targeted piece via `diagnostics.assembly.hoveredComponentId` or `selectedComponentId` and visual PNG inspection.
